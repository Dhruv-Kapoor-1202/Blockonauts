The Moon doesn't give you answers. It gives you consequences.

Near future. A robotic precursor found promising ground near the lunar south pole, and you've been picked as Junior Mission Commander. Limited mass, power, budget and time. Pack it, land it, run it. Every decision costs resources and time. There is no perfect answer.

Inspired by Reigns + Lapse: A Forgotten Future. A student-made experiment, not an official NASA product.
